## Decided
- The project is AGPL-3.0 with a commercial license option, which only works if the owner holds a license to every contribution.
- In the code: PR descriptions must include: "Concordo com a cessão de direitos descrita em CONTRIBUTING.md." Otherwise the PR is not merged.
